Gems are virtual items used inside the game. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn as money, and cannot be exchanged for real-world currency.

Important: Gems are intended only for use within the Tic Tac Toe game.

About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is an online multiplayer game focused on simple, fast and enjoyable matches. Players can compete, add friends, follow their statistics and complete daily challenges.

Optional virtual Gems can be used for features inside the game. Gems are not a financial product and have no monetary redemption value.
